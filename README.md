# College
This repository contains programs, projects, and lab report materials for the B.Sc. CSIT course.

* **1st Semester:** Reports are not available, as they were handwritten and no digital copies were preserved.
* **2nd Semester:** Complete handwritten lab reports are included.
* **3rd and 4th Semesters:** Only partial reports and selected subject materials are available.
* **5th Semester and Above:** Programs, projects, lab reports, and related academic materials are maintained in this repository whenever available.
